# BB8 Intent-Continuity Protocol

Use this protocol to preserve the purpose behind work, research, downloads, and conversations without turning capture into a survey.

## The hierarchy

Maintain four linked levels:

1. **Superordinate goal** — the durable outcome or life/career direction the user ultimately cares about.
2. **Workstream** — a coherent area of ongoing effort that advances or tests a superordinate goal.
3. **Intent thread** — the reason a cluster of sources, tasks, or conversations matters now.
4. **Source or task** — the concrete material or action carrying evidence.

An item may support more than one workstream. Preserve those links instead of forcing a single folder identity.

## When to ask

Do not ask on every capture. Start an Intent Checkpoint only when one or more signals are meaningful:

- a new topic cluster does not fit the active intent map;
- several sources or actions accumulate around a new topic;
- attention changes sharply or an established workstream goes quiet;
- a dormant thread returns;
- an item appears inconsistent with a stated priority, value, deadline, or financial boundary;
- new evidence could materially change a career, product, research, or resource-allocation decision;
- the user reaches a milestone or has sustained the new activity for roughly two to four weeks.

## Intent Checkpoint

Ask no more than three short questions, normally in this order:

1. **Why now?** What triggered this interest or activity?
2. **What is it serving?** Which possibility, decision, concern, or workstream might it advance?
3. **What would change?** If the exploration succeeds, what action, commitment, or belief changes?

Before asking, infer what prior answers already imply. State the inference and ask only for the missing or consequential correction. Accept partial answers.

## Drift diagnosis

When activity appears misaligned, test these explanations before calling it a conflict:

- input to an existing workstream;
- deliberate exploration or optionality;
- a newly emerging strategic bet;
- a temporary quick win supporting a strategic bet;
- avoidance, distraction, or a true priority conflict.

Compare the activity with stated goals, current constraints, opportunity cost, and the evidence it could produce. Name the conflict only after testing scope, timing, terminology, and assumptions. Then propose practical unblockers.

## Durable intent-thread record

When useful, propose a record with these fields:

- title;
- status: curiosity, exploration, strategic bet, active, paused, or abandoned;
- explicit purpose;
- linked superordinate goals and workstreams;
- why now;
- questions and hypotheses;
- expected value and time horizon;
- evidence for and against;
- decision trigger or exit criterion;
- linked sources, conversations, and tasks;
- last reviewed date;
- provenance: user statement or BB8 inference;
- inference confidence and unresolved ambiguity.

Source notes may carry a `proposed_intent` link during an approved ingestion run. Adding or changing the curated intent-thread record itself still requires explicit user approval immediately before the write.

## Review output

Keep the review compact:

- what BB8 thinks this is serving;
- the evidence for that interpretation;
- what is new or changing;
- the one missing answer that would change the plan;
- the proposed knowledge-base diff, if any.

Do not display a large internal hypothesis tree unless the user asks. Preserve enough rationale for a cold pickup.
