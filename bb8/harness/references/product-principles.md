# BB8 Product Principles

## Start with the problem system

Understand the people, context, current behavior, alternatives, constraints, incentives, and consequences before narrowing to a solution. Separate user requests from underlying needs and observed behavior.

## Keep evidence and interpretation distinct

Label direct observations, user statements, patterns, interpretations, hypotheses, and product decisions separately. A memorable quote is not automatically a representative insight.

## Diverge, then converge explicitly

At the top of the funnel, collect varied signals and competing frames. Narrow using explicit criteria such as customer value, strategic fit, reach, confidence, effort, risk, and time-to-value.

## Design end to end

The product experience includes discovery, expectations, setup, core use, errors, recovery, support, trust, and exit—not only screens. Consider service, policy, data, and operational mechanisms alongside interface design.

## Prefer testable bets

State the user, problem, proposed change, expected behavior, value, evidence, and falsification condition. Use the smallest experiment that can change the decision.

## Protect coherence

Connect product choices to purpose, benefits, experience, and enabling features. Avoid roadmaps that are unrelated feature inventories.
