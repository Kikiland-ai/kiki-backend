# BB8 Skill Catalog

## Command center

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `$bb8` | Executive framing, questions, Now/Next/Later planning, routing, and continuity. |

## Knowledge system

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `$organise` | Ingesting, preserving, structuring, retrieving, curating, and auditing the local knowledge base. |

## Reasoning and evidence

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `$deep-research` | Current, cited, multi-source investigation and synthesis. |
| `$data-analysis` | Quantitative analysis, segmentation, aggregation, disaggregation, and validation. |
| `$problem-solving` | Issue trees, hypotheses, Seven-Step analysis, Five Whys, fishbone, and unblockers. |
| `$minto-communication` | Answer-first executive narratives built from established analysis. |
| `$strategic-storytelling` | Purpose–benefit–experience–feature narratives and traceability audits. |
| `$transcript-insights-tofu` | Context reconstruction and evidence-backed findings from transcripts. |

## Product-design TOFU family

Use `$product-design` when you want the family router.

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `$product-discovery` | Users, problems, evidence, unmet needs, and opportunity maps. |
| `$product-strategy` | Strategic choices, positioning, portfolios, and product bets. |
| `$experience-design` | Journeys, interactions, service flows, states, trust, and recovery. |
| `$design-critique` | Prioritized critique of an existing product or service artifact. |
| `$experiment-design` | The smallest credible test that can change a product decision. |

## Routing rules

- Invoke a specific skill when the desired outcome is clear.
- Use a family router when the specialist route is unclear.
- Use BB8 when the work spans decisions, priorities, skills, or sessions.
- Use one primary and at most one supporting skill unless the work has distinct phases.
- Skills may activate implicitly when their description closely matches the request; family routers remain explicit-only.
