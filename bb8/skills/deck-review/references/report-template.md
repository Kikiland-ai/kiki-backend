# Report Template

Keep the report proportionate to the deck. Lead with the verdict, then the fix plan.
Show the full walk-through table only for decks over about 6 slides, or when asked;
otherwise summarise it in the verdict.

```markdown
# Deck review: <company> (<stage>, <sent|presented>, <business model>)

Assumptions: <anything inferred rather than given>

## Verdict
- **Investor's retell:** "<one sentence a partner would say to a colleague>"
- **Flip slide:** Slide <n>, <why interest turns to doubt here>
- **What they'd remember:** <the 3–4 points, or "none clear">
- **Do this first:** <the single next action>

## Fix plan

### P0: stops the read or breaks trust  (fix all of these first)
**Trust**
1. **<short title>** · Slides <n, m> · Evidence · Effort S
   - *Investor thinks:* "<first-person line>"
   - *Why it matters:* <principle> [<source key>]
   - *Fix:* <concrete change>

**Comprehension**
2. …

### P1: blocks conviction  (in reading order)
…grouped by Market and why now · Proof · Team · Ask…

### P2: adds friction
…

### P3: polish  (one design pass)
- <one line each>

## Prepare (off-deck)
Questions the investor will ask next, with the material to have ready:
- <question>: <cohort table / paid CAC by channel / COGS breakdown / …>

## Strengths to preserve
- <up to three, only real ones>

## Reader walk-through
| # | Slide headline | Investor thinks | State | Opens | Answers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | … | "…" | Interested | Q1 | — |

Open questions never answered: <Q-ids, which become findings above>

## Next step
Apply the fixes with deck-craft, starting with Storyline, then Evidence, then
Visual? <Yes/no question for the user>
```

Each finding's effort tag:
- **S:** reword, relabel, reorder, or delete. Minutes.
- **M:** rebuild a chart, rewrite a slide, rework sizing from existing data.
- **L:** needs new data, analysis, or a decision (for example the round size or a new market build).

Deck-craft tags: **Storyline** (§2), **Evidence** (§3), **Visual** (§4–7).
