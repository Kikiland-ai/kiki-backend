# Experience Design

Read [the shared product principles](../../harness/references/product-principles.md).

## Workflow

1. Define the user, context, goal, current journey, constraints, and desired benefit.
2. Identify moments of intent, decision, effort, uncertainty, trust, failure, and recovery.
3. Establish experience principles before choosing screens or components.
4. Generate alternative flows or service concepts, including non-interface mechanisms.
5. Specify the happy path, empty states, loading, errors, permissions, edge cases, recovery, and exit.
6. Check accessibility, comprehension, privacy, control, and operational feasibility.
7. Connect each major design choice to the benefit and evidence it serves.

## Output

Provide the journey or flow, key states, design rationale, assumptions, risks, open questions, and validation needs. Use diagrams or wireframes only when they improve understanding.

Do not optimize one screen while breaking the end-to-end experience.
