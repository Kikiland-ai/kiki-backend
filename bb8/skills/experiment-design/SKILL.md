# Experiment Design

Read [the shared product principles](../../harness/references/product-principles.md).

## Workflow

1. State the decision and uncertainty to reduce.
2. Form a falsifiable hypothesis with mechanism, target population, expected behavior, and value.
3. Choose the cheapest method capable of changing the decision: interview, prototype, concierge test, usability study, smoke test, pilot, or controlled experiment.
4. Define primary evidence, guardrails, segments, instrumentation, sample logic, and confounds.
5. Set decision thresholds, stopping rules, and what happens for positive, negative, or ambiguous results.
6. Plan synthesis that preserves negative cases and practical significance.

## Output

Provide the hypothesis, method, participants or population, procedure, measures, thresholds, risks, analysis plan, and decision table.

Do not run an experiment merely because measurement is possible. Avoid vanity metrics, underpowered certainty, peeking without rules, and changing the hypothesis after results arrive.
