# Curate mode

## Goal

Turn preserved sources into reusable knowledge without erasing provenance, disagreement, uncertainty, or the user's ownership of memory.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the decision or future use the curation should support.
2. Retrieve the relevant source notes and originals; do not synthesize from embeddings alone.
3. Separate sourced claims, user preferences, prior decisions, and BB8 inferences.
4. Identify candidate concepts, claims, frameworks, relationships, contradictions, and maps.
5. Search for an existing canonical note before proposing a new one.
6. Present a change set: create, amend, link, merge, archive, or no-op, with provenance and confidence.
7. Obtain explicit approval immediately before writing the curated changes.
8. Apply the approved diff without overwriting unrelated human content.
9. Validate backlinks and evidence paths, then record what changed.

Favor focused notes, but do not force artificial atomicity. Split when ideas have meaningfully independent reuse, evidence, lifecycle, or disagreement.
