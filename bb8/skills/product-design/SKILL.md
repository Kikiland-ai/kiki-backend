# Product Design Router

Clarify the product decision, funnel stage, users, evidence, constraints, and desired artifact. Route to one primary specialist:

- `$product-discovery` for broad problem and opportunity discovery.
- `$product-strategy` for choices, positioning, opportunity portfolios, and strategic bets.
- `$experience-design` for journeys, interaction concepts, service flows, and states.
- `$design-critique` for evidence-based evaluation of an existing concept or artifact.
- `$experiment-design` for tests that reduce product uncertainty.
- `$strategic-storytelling` when the core need is purpose-to-feature traceability.

Use at most one supporting specialist unless the work has distinct phases. Preserve customer evidence, assumptions, non-goals, constraints, and the decision to be unlocked.

Do not jump from an ambiguous problem directly to polished interface production.
