# Product Discovery

Read [the shared product principles](../../harness/references/product-principles.md).

## Workflow

1. Define the discovery boundary, target actors, strategic context, and decision horizon.
2. Gather signals from behavior, interviews, support, usage, market alternatives, operations, and constraints.
3. Separate observations, needs, causes, desired outcomes, solution requests, and hypotheses.
4. Map journeys, jobs, frictions, workarounds, anxieties, and switching conditions.
5. Cluster patterns without erasing meaningful segment differences or negative cases.
6. Form opportunity statements and competing problem frames.
7. Narrow only after defining evidence, value, strategic fit, confidence, risk, and time-to-value criteria.

## Output

Provide the context model, evidence-backed needs, opportunity map, segments or situations, unresolved contradictions, candidate opportunities, and next research questions.

Do not count mentions as importance without context. Do not treat requested features as validated needs. Preserve provenance for material findings.
