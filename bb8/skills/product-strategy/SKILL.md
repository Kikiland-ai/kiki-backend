# Product Strategy

Read [the shared product principles](../../harness/references/product-principles.md).

## Workflow

1. Establish the purpose, constraints, assets, market or ecosystem, and decision horizon.
2. Identify target users and situations, alternatives, unmet value, and sources of advantage.
3. Generate materially different strategic choices and explicit non-goals.
4. Test coherence across customer value, differentiation, capabilities, economics, distribution, risk, and time-to-value.
5. Classify initiatives as Strategic Bets, Quick Wins, Fillers, or Money Pits.
6. Define assumptions, leading indicators, validation milestones, and exit criteria.
7. Connect the chosen portfolio to product principles and downstream discovery or delivery.

## Output

Present the strategic question, evidence, choices and tradeoffs, opportunity portfolio, assumptions, indicators, and decisions required. Leave the final choice to the user unless asked to choose.

Do not label a goal, roadmap, or feature list as strategy without exclusions and a theory of advantage.
