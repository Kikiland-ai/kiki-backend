# Transcript Insights TOFU

Convert raw conversation into traceable learning without collapsing nuance too early.

Read [the pipeline](references/pipeline.md) for staged analysis and [the findings rubric](references/findings-rubric.md) when converting signals into findings.

## Workflow

1. Establish the research or meeting context, participants, purpose, decisions, and known limitations.
2. Normalize transcript structure while preserving speaker, sequence, timestamps, and source identity.
3. Reconstruct the conversation's context, terminology, goals, constraints, and turning points.
4. Extract observations, quotes, decisions, actions, questions, assumptions, tensions, needs, workarounds, and contradictions.
5. Cluster related signals without losing source provenance or negative cases.
6. Compare across speakers, segments, sessions, and time where available.
7. Develop findings only when a pattern has a mechanism, significance, and sufficient evidence.
8. Separate findings, hypotheses, opportunities, recommendations, and follow-up questions.

## Output

Include a context brief, decision and action ledger, themes, evidence-backed findings, tensions or contradictions, opportunities, confidence, and next research questions. Link material findings back to transcript evidence.

## Boundaries

- Do not fabricate quotations or speaker intent.
- Do not treat frequency as importance without context.
- Do not erase disagreement by averaging perspectives.
- Do not confuse a participant request with the underlying need.
- Do not recommend before showing the evidence and reasoning that support the recommendation.
