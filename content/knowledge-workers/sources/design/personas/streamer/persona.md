## Goals

1. Hit the agency's launch-month target (illustrative: average CCV 550). Her Day 2 stream averaged 509.
2. Build a run-of-show that holds viewers through slow stretches: grinds, loading, menus.
3. Pitch next week's plan to her agency manager clearly, in two lines.

## Pains

- The dashboard shows what happened, not why.
- Agency feedback arrives as English bullet points on a weekly call.
- Launch week is crowded: everyone streams the same game, and browsing viewers hop between streams.
- She goes quiet when she concentrates; grinds and loading turn into dead air.
- She changes five things at once and can't tell which one worked.

## The scenario in one paragraph

Launch week, Day 2: a 3-hour stream from 20:00 IST, peak 682 CCV at 2:03:00, average 509. At 38:00 she ended a story mission and started a silent cash grind: mic mostly off, no goal said, chat not read. **Chat fell first**, from 87 messages a minute at 37:00 to 15 at 41:00. CCV held at 556 until 40:00, then fell to **348 by 59:00 (−37%)**, with 1 follow in the whole segment. From 60:00 she ran two chat polls and talked through a plan, and CCV recovered to 477 by 91:00 (segment retention 1.36 against the grind's 0.63). An incoming raid at 95:00 and a collab took her to the peak. Generator and checks: [scenario.json](scenario.json).

## Language notes (machine draft)

- Romanised Hinglish, casual: "arre", "yaar", "chalo". Meera talks like a streamer; KiKi talks like a friendly coach, not a co-streamer.
- Terms kept in English and shown as chips: CCV, retention, chat rate, chat loop, raid, VOD, hook, segment, poll, run-of-show.
- Proposed language code: `hi-Latn` (Hindi in Latin script). Contract §0 lists ISO 639-1 codes only, so the hub should confirm it.
- Numbers and times keep one form everywhere (38:00, 556, −37%, 0.63).
