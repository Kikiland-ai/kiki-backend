Kiki's static artwork, for slides, the pitch deck, app icons and anywhere the live `KikiPointer` component can't run.

- `kiki-pointer-<state>.svg`: the pointer form in each of its 11 states (idle, pointing, tapping, dragging, tracing, thinking, asking, listening, celebrating, unsure, waiting). Each file is 160 × 160 with room around the cursor. The cursor tip sits at (12, 8) in the 80-unit drawing.
- `kiki-dots.svg`: the original four-dot form, for small marks, the avatar and loading states.
- `kiki-pointer-sheet.svg`: all 11 states on one labelled sheet, for reviews.

Inks: the cursor body is #1f1f1f with a #ffffff outline in every theme, so it reads on paper, scope glass and screenshots alike. The dots are always blue #4285f4, red #ea4335, yellow #fbbc05 and green #34a853, in that order. In tapping and waiting, the ring takes the colour of the skill in play (green in the lab).
