# Snackbar

A short dark status message at the bottom of the screen, such as "Rerouting… +1 stop, 6 min" or "No signal. Your progress is saved on this phone."

**Consumer provides:** `message`, optional `tone` (`route`: a blue route-line mark), `icon`, `actionLabel` with `onAction`.

- Do: say what happened and what it means for the student.
- Don't: show error codes or apologise.
